Swan & Pond Interactive Physics Banner

Handcrafted multi-layer animation preserved from portfolio

Interactive Features Active:
  • Mouse Tracking: Move your mouse across the banner — the right swan actively swims toward the cursor and banks with velocity, while the left swan gently turns.
  • Interactive Ripples: Move cursor slowly across water to generate water ripples; click anywhere to cast a strong ripple.
  • Trailing Wake: The active swimming swan spawns wake ripples behind it as it surges forward.
  • Theme Switching: Click the button above to toggle between deep moonlit night and watercolor day palette.
